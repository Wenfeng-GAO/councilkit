## Bug 描述 (description)

Under buffer pressure (>LIMITS.executionBufferBytes = 32MiB), bufferEvent appends the consolidated output.snapshot at the tail of `kept` with seq=firstDeltaSeq, while follow() replays record.events in storage order with no seq sort, so a client resuming via GET /api/v1/scopes/:scopeId/executions/:executionId/events receives a low-seq output.snapshot after higher-seq activity/usage events — violating the ordered-stream and afterSeq-resume contract.

## 生产可达性 (production reachability)

- **entrypoint**: `Host SSE execution-resume path (ExecutionRegistry.follow afterSeq) used when a client reconnects mid-execution`
- **call chain**:
  - driver streams interleaved output.delta and activity/usage events through ExecutionRegistry.emit into record.events in arrival order
  - bufferEvent, under LIMITS.executionBufferBytes (32 MiB) pressure, collects every output.delta/output.snapshot into one consolidated output.snapshot and pushes it at the TAIL of kept (after all kept non-delta activity events) with seq=firstDeltaSeq
  - a resuming client calling follow(executionId, afterSeq=0) iterates record.events in storage order and receives the low-seq consolidated snapshot AFTER activity events that actually occurred later, violating monotonic seq delivery

## 违反的契约 (violated contract)

ExecutionRegistry.follow must replay buffered events to a resuming client in monotonically increasing seq order; coalescing under buffer pressure must not move early streamed output behind later activity/tool events. The registry docstring promises strictly-greater-than afterSeq resume, which clients rely on for an ordered transcript.

## 根因 (root cause)

Coalescing appends the consolidated output.snapshot to the tail of `kept` instead of splicing it in at firstDeltaSeq's position, and follow replays record.events in storage order without sorting by seq, so the low-seq snapshot is delivered after higher-seq activity events.

## 修复方向 (suggested fix)

Either splice the consolidated snapshot into kept at the index of the first replaced delta/snapshot (preserving its seq position), or sort record.events by seq before replay in follow (and re-sort after each coalescing). Keep seq=firstDeltaSeq so afterSeq skip semantics remain correct. Add an invariant assertion that record.events is seq-sorted after bufferEvent.
