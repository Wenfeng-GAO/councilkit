    2) 导出 `function mapStreamErrorToGatewayError(input: {httpStatus?: number; errorCode?: string; errorMessage: string}): GatewayError`：
       - httpStatus === 401 || 403 → {kind:'invalid_key', message:errorMessage, httpStatus}
       - httpStatus === 429 → {kind:'rate_limit', message:errorMessage, httpStatus:429}
       - httpStatus >= 500 && < 600 → {kind:'upstream', message:errorMessage, httpStatus}
       - errorCode === 'timeout' → {kind:'timeout', message:errorMessage}（无 httpStatus）
       - else → {kind:'network', message:errorMessage}

    3) 导出 `function anthropicAdapter(params: {baseUrl: string; apiKey: string; model: string; messages: ModelMessage[]; signal?: AbortSignal}): AsyncIterable<string | GatewayError>`：
